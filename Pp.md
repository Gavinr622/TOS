2. Information We Collect

Depending on which features you use, Overload AI may collect or process the following information.

2.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we may store:

- Username.
- Password hash.
- Password salt.
- Account creation date.
- Account access expiration information.
- Linked Discord account ID.

Passwords should be stored as password hashes rather than readable passwords.

2.2 Discord Information

When you use Discord authentication or the Discord bot, we may process:

- Discord user ID.
- Discord username and display name.
- Discord server IDs.
- Discord server names.
- Information necessary to link your Discord account.
- Information received through Discord OAuth2 permissions.

We do not need your Discord password to authenticate your account.

2.3 Messages and Conversation Data

When you interact with Overload AI, we may process:

- Messages you send to the bot.
- AI-generated responses.
- Conversation memory.
- Queued messages.
- Message identifiers.
- Discord channel and server identifiers needed to process messages.

Messages may be sent to the configured AI model server to generate responses.

2.4 Usage Statistics

We may collect usage information such as:

- Number of messages processed.
- Message timestamps.
- Server usage statistics.
- Activity information.
- Leaderboard statistics.
- Service usage events.

These statistics help operate the Service and provide dashboard features.

2.5 Security and Login Information

For account security, we may store:

- Hashed login session tokens.
- Session expiration timestamps.
- Login history.
- Verification records.
- Security events.
- Information used to detect suspicious activity.

We may also process basic technical information needed to protect the website and its accounts.

Overload AI may use Discord OAuth2 for account authentication and account linking.

Depending on the permissions you approve, the Service may access:

- Your Discord account identity.
- Your Discord server list.
- Other information covered by the requested OAuth2 permissions.

You can manage authorized applications through your Discord account settings.

6. Cookies and Login Sessions

The website may use cookies to maintain authenticated sessions.

These cookies may:

- Keep you signed in.
- Identify your active login session.
- Help protect account access.

Session tokens should be stored securely, and website cookies may use security settings such as HttpOnly, SameSite, and Secure when supported by the connection.

Logging out may invalidate the associated session.

Using the log-out-all-devices feature, where available, may invalidate all active sessions for your account.

7. Free Access and Abuse Prevention

Overload AI may provide eligible users with a temporary introductory access pass.

To prevent repeated redemption, we may maintain a separate record associated with a Discord account identifier.

This record may remain after an account is deleted.

Important: Deleting an account does not necessarily delete the minimal identifier retained to enforce one-time introductory access eligibility.

This information is retained for abuse prevention and is not intended to serve as a normal user profile.

13. Account and Data Deletion

Overload AI may provide several deletion options.

Delete Chat Data

This option may remove saved conversation memory and queued messages while keeping your account.

Delete Account

This option may remove your account, login sessions, and linked account association.

Some separate security and abuse-prevention records may remain.

Delete All Data

This option may remove eligible account information, conversation memory, queued messages, and associated records.

Certain minimal records may still be retained where reasonably necessary for abuse prevention, security, or legal compliance.

Deleting your Overload AI account does not automatically delete:

- Messages already sent through Discord.
- Information held by Discord.
- Information held by independent third-party services.
- Records that must be retained for legitimate security or legal purposes.
